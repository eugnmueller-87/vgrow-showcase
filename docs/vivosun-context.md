# A contribution toward VIVOSUN's stated direction

[Product overview](../README.md) · [Local AI](ai-models.md) · [Roadmap](roadmap.md)

The SGS 3.0 direction shown in the supplied screenshot is a useful starting point for a collaboration conversation. It describes connected equipment managed with local intelligence and less recurring intervention.

VIVOSUN's own [Smart Grow System page](https://www.vivosun.com/en-US/smart-grow-system) presents SGS 3.0 as unified local AI-assisted management, coordinated environmental devices and adaptation to the growing environment. The [X42 manual](https://www.vivosun.com/support/guide/growhub-x42) documents AI climate functions, operating strategies and logs; it says advanced AI activation/configuration uses the app. These are manufacturer descriptions, not results of our testing an X42.

That means the pitch should acknowledge their existing direction. It should not say VIVOSUN has no automation or AI, that we invented local growing intelligence, or that the X42 has failed to deliver its claims. Our experience and verified integration are with a **VGrow Y18**, not an X42 evaluation.

## What our installation demonstrates

| Product direction | Our verified contribution | Still to establish |
|---|---|---|
| An easier everyday experience | Readings, explanations, two cameras, records and knowledge in one workspace | Usability and reliability with independent first-time users |
| Local intelligence | Own-host Gemma language/vision and Granite retrieval integrated with application evidence | No claim that our language models are equivalent to the X42's climate AI |
| Coordinated care | Deterministic climate logic, light timer, pump timer and need checks | Complete live timer setup and physical calibration; no learned recipe optimization yet |
| A connected equipment system | One connected Y18 controller plus separate camera sources and multiple records | More real controllers and protocol-specific adapters |
| Less cloud dependence | AI inference, stored records and application decisions run on the owner's host | Current VGrow commands and reports still depend on the manufacturer's cloud |
| Improvement across grows | Persistent observations, source-labelled records, history and export | Validated learning from repeated grows; history alone is not model training |

The local language/vision layer and the deterministic control layer have different jobs. Running a language model nearby does not by itself prove adaptive control, automatic recipe learning or offline device operation.

## The collaboration proposition

**“Your SGS 3.0 direction matches the problem I experienced as a VGrow owner. I built a working cockpit on my own hardware that connects daily routines, camera evidence, plant history and local AI explanations. I would like to help develop and validate that experience with your team.”**

The opportunity is to contribute a working prototype, owner insight and implementation experience. A pilot can establish useful additions, integration requirements and a possible product-development role. It need not start with distributing private source or claiming superiority over untested hardware.
